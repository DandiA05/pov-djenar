Last updated: November 25, 2026

POV Djenar respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our website and related services.

Our website does not directly collect personal information through forms or user registration. If you authorize our application to access your YouTube account, Google may provide information necessary to perform the requested functionality.

2. YouTube API Services

Our application uses YouTube API Services to support authorized YouTube video upload functionality. Access is granted only after users provide consent.

Our use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data Usage

Authorized data is used only to provide requested YouTube functionality. We do not sell personal information or use Google user data for advertising.

4. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect authentication credentials and authorized data against unauthorized access.

5. Third-Party Services

Our application interacts with Google and YouTube services, which are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the POV Djenar channel owner through the associated YouTube channel.
